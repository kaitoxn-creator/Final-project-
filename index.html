Walking in the Light

A TCGC student's reflections on faith, integrity, and being a light online.

Welcome

I am Issaiah Mosses A. Caparoso, a student of Tangub City Global College. This website is my personal reflection on how faith guides what I do in school and online.

At TCGC, Una ang Ginoo (God First) is more than a motto. It is the first question I ask before I study, post, or reply: does this honor God and respect the people who will read it? As a follower of the Light of the World, I want my words, my work, and even my web pages to bring clarity and hope to others.

Reflection Essay: Walking in the Light

When I first learned that my final project would be a website, I thought it was only about code. Tags, colors and layouts were the tools. But as I built this page I realized that every choice I make online says something about who I am and what I believe. For me, technology is not separate from faith. It is another place where I can choose to be a light.

My faith begins with Una ang Ginoo, putting God first. At Tangub City Global College, this is not just a phrase on a wall. It is a daily decision. When I pray before a quiz, I am not asking for a shortcut; I am asking for a calm mind and an honest heart. When I am tired or discouraged, I remember that my effort is a way of thanking Him for the chance to learn. Putting God first reminds me that my grades and achievements are not the center of my life. They are gifts to be used well and shared with others.

From faith comes integrity. Integrity means I am the same person whether a teacher is watching or not whether I am in the classroom or alone with my phone. It is easy to copy an answer or to ignore a mistake but each small compromise teaches my heart to compromise again. I want to be a student whose word can be trusted. When I admit a mistake, give credit to someone else's work, or refuse to join in gossip I practice what I believe and I become the kind of person others can rely on.

The same values apply to digital citizenship. The internet gives each of us a voice that can reach hundreds of people in seconds, and that power carries responsibility. A single comment can encourage a classmate or wound them for a long time. A single shared rumor can damage someone's name. So I try to pause before I post and ask whether it is true, kind, and necessary. I try to verify before I share and respect other people's privacy and work. Being a good digital citizen is simply loving my neighbor in the places where we meet online.

Jesus called His followers the light of the world, and said that a light should not be hidden. A light is not meant to show off; it is meant to help people see. This is what I hope this website does. I hope it shows how faith, integrity, and responsible technology can work together to inspire and uplift others. I know I will make mistakes, but each day is a new chance to begin again. If my words, my work, and my web pages can bring even one person a little hope, then I have walked in the light.

— Issaiah Mosses A. Caparoso, TCGC

Faith is not only for Sunday. When I pray before a quiz or a project, I remember that my effort is also a way of thanking Him. Jesus said He is the light of the world, and He invites His followers to carry that light to others. At TCGC, I want to carry it through how I study, serve, and speak.

Prayer

📖

🌟

Gratitude

I thank God for my family, teachers, classmates, and the chance to learn.

🤝

I use my skills, even my computer skills, to help others and honor Him.

Page 4 · Integrity

Integrity: One Person Everywhere

Being honest and consistent in class, at home, and online, even when no one is watching.

What integrity means to me

Integrity is being the same person in every place and in every moment.

It is the first value on the TCGC seal. It means my words match my actions, I do the right thing when it is hard, and I take responsibility for my mistakes. A person of integrity can be trusted, and trust is a light that others can follow.

Four parts of integrity

⚖️

Honesty

I tell the truth and do my own work, even when a shortcut is easy.

🤝

Accountability

I admit my mistakes and fix them instead of blaming others.

🧭

Consistency

I act the same online, offline, and when I think no one sees.

💪

Courage

I say no to cheating and bullying, even if friends are doing it.

“The integrity of the upright shall guide them.”Proverbs 11:3

Integrity in school

Test your choices

I copied code from a friend. What do I do?

I tell my teacher honestly redo the work in my own way and learn the lesson. Honesty now protects my future.

I see a classmate's private photo being shared. What do I do?

I do not forward it. I tell the person and report it to a teacher so the harm stops.

My integrity pledge

I promise to be honest in my work, to own my mistakes, and to be the same person everywhere. Una ang Ginoo.

Page 5 · Digital Citizenship

Responsible Digital Citizenship

Five habits that keep my online life honest and kind.

Why it matters

A Global Collegian carries the Light of the World everywhere, including online.

Words on the internet last longer than words spoken, and they can reach thousands of people in seconds. That power is a responsibility. I want my posts, comments, and projects to inform, encourage, and protect people.

My five habits

1. Think before posting

I ask three questions: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it necessary? If one answer is no, I do not post.

2. Verify before sharing

I check the source, date, and author. Sharing a rumor, even by accident, can hurt real people.

3. Give credit

I name the creator of images, music, and ideas. Honesty in digital creation is part of integrity.

4. Protect privacy

I do not post other people's photos or personal details without permission, and I guard my own information.

5. Respond with grace

When someone is rude, I answer calmly or choose not to answer. A gentle reply can end an argument.

What would a light-bearer do?

💬

A classmate is being mocked in a group chat

I do not join in. I message the classmate privately, and I report the bullying to a teacher if it continues.

📰

A shocking post is going viral

I search for a reliable source first. If it is false, I do not share it, and I kindly correct those who did.
