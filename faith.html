Reflection 1

Faith and Integrity

What it means to put God first when no one is watching.

Una ang Ginoo

Putting God first means making Him my starting point, not an afterthought.

When I pray before a quiz or a project, I am reminded that my effort is also a way of thanking Him. Faith is not only for Sunday; it is how I treat my classmates, my teachers, and my own responsibilities every day.

Three values, one light

The TCGC seal carries three words: Integrity, Compassion, and Excellence. Here is how I try to live each one.

⚖️

Integrity

What it means: being honest and consistent. How I live it: I do my own work, give credit to others, and tell the truth even when it costs me.

❤️

Compassion

What it means: caring for people, not just tasks. How I live it: I notice classmates who struggle, share what I know, and speak kindly.

⭐

Excellence

What it means: doing my best as an offering. How I live it: I prepare well and finish what I start, because my talents are gifts.

A day of walking in the light

Morning

I start with a short prayer and ask God to guide my choices and my words.

At school

I stay honest in quizzes and projects, and I help a classmate when I can.

Online

I pause before posting and choose words that lift people up.

Evening

I thank God and review where I shone and where I need to grow.

“Let your light so shine before men.”Matthew 5:16

My reflection

Integrity is being the same person in the classroom, at home, and online. The Light of the World calls me to shine in small, ordinary choices, not only in big moments. When I choose honesty over a shortcut, I am already letting His light show.

Una ang Ginoo. Light in the web, light in the world.
