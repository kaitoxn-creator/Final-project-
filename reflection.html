Welcome

I am Issaiah Mosses A. Caparoso, a student of Tangub City Global College. This website is my personal reflection on how faith guides what I do in school and online.

At TCGC, Una ang Ginoo (God First) is more than a motto. It is the first question I ask before I study, post, or reply: does this honor God and respect the people who will read it? As a follower of the Light of the World, I want my words, my work, and even my web pages to bring clarity and hope to others.

About this project

Light in the Web is a value-centered website that shows technology and faith working together. It has four sections: my faith and integrity, my habits as a digital citizen, and a private journal where visitors write their own reflections.
