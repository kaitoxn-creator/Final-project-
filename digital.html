Reflection 2

Responsible Digital Citizenship

Five habits that keep my online life honest and kind.

Why it matters

A Global Collegian carries the Light of the World everywhere, including online.

Words on the internet last longer than words spoken, and they can reach thousands of people in seconds. That power is a responsibility. I want my posts, comments, and projects to inform, encourage, and protect people.

My five habits

1. Think before posting

I ask three questions: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it necessary? If one answer is no, I do not post.

2. Verify before sharing

I check the source, date, and author. Sharing a rumor, even by accident, can hurt real people.

3. Give credit

I name the creator of images, music, and ideas. Honesty in digital creation is part of integrity.

4. Protect privacy

I do not post other people's photos or personal details without permission, and I guard my own information.

5. Respond with grace

When someone is rude, I answer calmly or choose not to answer. A gentle reply can end an argument.

What would a light-bearer do?

💬

A classmate is being mocked in a group chat

I do not join in. I message the classmate privately, and I report the bullying to a teacher if it continues.

📰

A shocking post is going viral

I search for a reliable source first. If it is false, I do not share it, and I kindly correct those who did.
